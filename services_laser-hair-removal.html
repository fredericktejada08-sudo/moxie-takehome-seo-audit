Laser Hair Removal

long-lasting hair reduction

Smooth Skin Without Constant Maintenance

Laser hair removal is a cosmetic procedure that uses concentrated pulses of light to target and destroy hair follicles at the source. The laser energy focuses on melanin within the hair while protecting the surrounding skin. Common treatment areas include the chin, upper lip, chest, underarms, back, bikini line, and legs, offering a long-term solution to unwanted hair.

safe & effective technology

Precision Treatment for Lasting Results

The handheld device delivers intense laser pulses across the treatment area, targeting individual follicles during their active growth (anagen) stage. Because hair grows in cycles, multiple sessions—typically three to five spaced several weeks apart—are necessary for optimal results. For approximately 90% of patients, results are permanent or long-lasting, with regrowth appearing finer and lighter.

treatment & recovery

Procedure & Healing Timeline

Treatments are generally quick and well-tolerated, with most patients describing the sensation as a mild snapping feeling against the skin. Temporary redness, swelling, or mild soreness may occur but typically resolves within a few days. Cold compresses, moisturizers, and sun protection help support recovery.

treatment benefits

Why Choose Laser Hair Removal

Laser hair removal offers a more permanent and less irritating alternative to shaving, waxing, or depilatory creams. The procedure is safe for most body areas (excluding the eye area) and provides precise follicle targeting with minimal disruption to surrounding tissue.

post-treatment care

Caring for Your Skin After Treatment

Proper aftercare helps reduce irritation and protect treated skin while achieving the best possible results. Mild redness or sensitivity is normal and temporary.

Post-Treatment DO's:

Apply sunscreen daily to protect healing skin.

Keep skin moisturized.

Apply cold compresses if needed.

Post-Treatment DON'Ts:

Avoid sun exposure immediately after.

Do not wax between sessions.

Avoid hot tubs or saunas for 24 hours.

Do not pick at irritated skin.

FAQ

Any Questions?

Am I a good candidate for laser hair removal?

Patients with dark hair and lighter skin typically respond best, though newer technologies have improved outcomes for a wider range of skin tones.

How many sessions will I need?

Most patients require three to five treatments spaced 4–8 weeks apart, depending on hair texture and location.

Is laser hair removal painful?

The procedure is usually well-tolerated. Some patients describe a sensation similar to a rubber band snapping against the skin.

What are possible complications from laser hair removal?

Mild irritation, temporary pigment changes, or regrowth may occur. Regrown hair is often finer and lighter and can be treated with additional sessions.
