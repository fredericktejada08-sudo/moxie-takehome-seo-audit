About Us

welcome to muse!

Combining Art & Science for Optimal Skin Health

Welcome to Muse MedSpa in Austin, Texas, where advanced aesthetics and artistry support healthy, confident skin. We believe aesthetic care should feel personal, thoughtful, and rooted in trust. From consultation to treatment, our team guides you with education, intention, and genuine connection.

At Muse MedSpa, you are more than a client—you are part of our community. We specialize in non-invasive treatments tailored to your goals, including skin rejuvenation, laser services, body contouring, and injectables for men and women.

Our focus is your comfort and confidence, with results that feel natural and balanced. We invest in proven technology and refined techniques to ensure every recommendation reflects the highest standards of safety and quality.

Through continued education and advanced training, Muse MedSpa providers remain at the forefront of aesthetic medicine. We are committed to delivering elevated care that helps you look refreshed and beautifully yourself.

who is Sydney Scott-Sullivan?

About Sydney

Muse MedSpa founder and aesthetic nurse Injector with over 23 years of experience and clinical precision.

Our founder, Sydney Scott-Sullivan, has dedicated her career to the science of skin and the art of aesthetics. Trained in Medical Aesthetics both in the United States and internationally, she has worked alongside leading Plastic Surgery and Dermatology practices before founding Muse MedSpa.

Recognized as a RealSelf Top Injector, Sydney continues advanced education through elite trainings and masterclasses. She holds certifications with respected aesthetic organizations and remains committed to innovative techniques that support natural, longer-lasting results.

Sydney believes in personalized treatment plans built on education, trust, and integrity. Her goal is to help every client feel confident, refreshed, and beautifully themselves.
